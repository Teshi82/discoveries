Travel essentials

Etiquette

Avoid phone calls on trains. At a hot spring, wash before entering the bath and keep your towel out of the water.

There are no difficult rules. The basic idea is consideration: not getting in the way of the people around you. When in doubt, just do as those around you do.

Key points

  1. On trains and buses

    Avoid phone calls and keep conversation quiet. Carry backpacks in front of you, and line up to board.

  2. Taking off your shoes

    Remove your shoes at the entrance of ryokan inns, temples and homes. In tatami rooms, take off slippers as well.

  3. Rubbish

    There are very few bins on the streets. Carry a small bag and take your rubbish back to your accommodation.

  4. Onsen and public baths

    Wash before getting into the bath, and keep your towel out of the water. Photography in bathing areas is prohibited.

Trip notes

Travel tip

Do not photograph people or private property without permission. In Kyoto's Gion district, some private lanes are closed to entry and photography.
