Travel essentials

Getting around

The Shinkansen is fast and punctual between cities. A rechargeable IC card covers trains, buses and even small purchases.

Japan's railways form a dense network and run on time. The basic pattern is Shinkansen between cities, and trains and subways within them.

Key points

  1. Shinkansen

    Tokyo to Shin-Osaka takes about 2 hours 30 minutes at the fastest. Reserved seats can be booked at station counters and ticket machines or online.

  2. IC cards

    Suica, ICOCA and similar cards work across Japan's major cities and can also be added to a smartphone.

  3. Japan Rail Pass

    A pass for short-term visitors giving unlimited travel on JR lines. Riding Nozomi and Mizuho trains requires an extra fee.

  4. Highway buses and domestic flights

    Overnight buses are handy for keeping costs down. For Hokkaido, Kyushu and Okinawa, flying is faster.

Trip notes

Travel tip

Weekday mornings from about 7:30 to 9:30 are rush hour. Avoid travelling with large luggage at that time.
