Explore by season

The same place becomes a different landscape depending on when you visit.

Following the cherry blossom front

Cherry blossoms open in Kyushu in late March and reach Hokkaido by early May. Timing shifts every year, so check the latest bloom forecast.

Best time
Late March – early May
Where to go
Mt. Yoshino (Nara) · Hirosaki Park (Aomori) · Meguro River (Tokyo)

Festivals, fireworks and cool highlands

Fireworks light up the night sky and festivals fill the streets. To escape the heat, head for higher ground or further north — Kamikochi or Hokkaido.

Best time
July – August
Where to go
Kamikochi (Nagano) · Aomori Nebuta Festival · Furano (Hokkaido)

Autumn colours descending from the mountains

Autumn colours begin in the mountains of Hokkaido and move slowly south. Kyoto's temple gardens turn red and gold around late November.

Best time
October – early December
Where to go
Nikko (Tochigi) · Tofuku-ji (Kyoto) · Oirase Stream (Aomori)

Snowscapes and hot springs

Snow piled on thatched farmhouses, and open-air baths with a view of it falling. With its clear air, winter is also the best season for seeing Mt. Fuji.

Best time
December – February
Where to go
Shirakawa-go (Gifu) · Niseko (Hokkaido) · Ginzan Onsen (Yamagata)

More than scenery

Beyond the views, you'll find living traditions, cutting-edge culture and the flavours of each region.

Food

Sushi, ramen, regional cooking. Taste the seasons and the local produce, from street stalls to century-old restaurants.

Traditional culture

Castles, shrines and temples, kimono, festivals. Experience a sense of beauty handed down over generations.

Pop culture

Anime, games and the latest technology. The city itself is the entertainment.

Hot springs

Hot springs well up all over the country. Bathe with a view of the snow, or meet wild monkeys soaking in the water.

Travel essentials

Getting around

The Shinkansen is fast and punctual between cities. A rechargeable IC card covers trains, buses and even small purchases.

Payment

Cards and mobile payments are widely accepted, but small rural shops, temples and shrines may take cash only.

Etiquette

Avoid phone calls on trains. At a hot spring, wash before entering the bath and keep your towel out of the water.

Staying connected

An eSIM or pocket Wi‑Fi is convenient. Free Wi‑Fi is also available at airports and major stations.
