Travel essentials

Staying connected

An eSIM or pocket Wi‑Fi is convenient. Free Wi‑Fi is also available at airports and major stations.

Maps, route planners, translation: your smartphone is your best friend on the road. Decide how you will get online before you leave, so it works the moment you arrive.

Key points

  1. eSIM

    Buy and set it up online before departure and you can use it as soon as you land. Check that your device is compatible.

  2. Pocket Wi-Fi

    One unit connects several devices, so it suits groups and families. You can pick it up and return it at the airport.

  3. Free Wi-Fi

    Available at airports, major stations, hotels and cafés. Signal can be weak in the mountains and on remote islands.

  4. Power

    The voltage is 100 V and plugs are Type A, with two flat pins. Check whether you need an adapter.

Trip notes

Travel tip

Save map and translation app data for offline use, just in case. Photos and maps drain the battery quickly, so bring a power bank too.
