The Golden Route

For a first trip to Japan: the classic route combining city energy, Mt. Fuji and the ancient capitals.

The classic route west from Tokyo along the Shinkansen line. With few transfers, it is easy to plan even on a first visit. Flying home from Kansai International Airport saves the trip back to Tokyo.

Itinerary

  1. Tokyo (days 1–3)

    Explore old and new neighbourhoods: Asakusa, Shibuya, the Imperial Palace area. Keep your arrival day light to allow for jet lag.

  2. Hakone and Mt. Fuji (day 4)

    About 1 hour 25 minutes from Shinjuku to Hakone-Yumoto on the Romancecar limited express. Stay at a hot-spring inn; on a clear day Mt. Fuji rises beyond Lake Ashi.

  3. Kyoto (days 5–6)

    About 2 hours from Odawara by Shinkansen. See Kinkaku-ji, Fushimi Inari, Higashiyama and more, one district at a time.

  4. Nara (afternoon of day 6)

    About 45 minutes by train from Kyoto. A half-day trip to the Great Buddha of Todai-ji and the deer of Nara Park.

  5. Osaka (day 7)

    About 30 minutes by train from Kyoto. Eat your way along Dotonbori, then head home from Kansai International Airport.

Trip notes

Best time
Late March to April for cherry blossoms and October to November for autumn leaves are the most popular. Summers are hot and humid.
Getting around
The whole route can be done by Shinkansen and local trains. Compare the price of a Japan Rail Pass with individual tickets before you decide.

Travel tip

Send large bags ahead to your next hotel by courier and travel light (delivery is usually next day). Bringing oversized luggage onto the Shinkansen requires a seat reservation in advance.
