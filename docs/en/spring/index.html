Explore by season

Spring

Following the cherry blossom front

Cherry blossoms open in Kyushu in late March and reach Hokkaido by early May. Timing shifts every year, so check the latest bloom forecast.

Highlights

  1. Mt. Yoshino (Nara)

    Some 30,000 cherry trees bloom in sequence from the foot of the mountain to the top. Best from early to mid-April.

  2. Hirosaki Park (Aomori)

    Known for the cherry trees around Hirosaki Castle and for the "flower rafts" of fallen petals that cover the moat. Best around late April.

  3. Meguro River (Tokyo)

    About 800 cherry trees line roughly 4 km of riverbank in the heart of the city. Best from late March to early April.

  4. Hitachi Seaside Park (Ibaraki)

    From mid-April to early May, nemophila flowers turn a whole hillside blue. A spring scene to catch after the cherry blossoms.

Trip notes

Best time
Late March – early May
Where to go
Mt. Yoshino (Nara) · Hirosaki Park (Aomori) · Meguro River (Tokyo)
What to wear
Mornings and evenings are chilly, so a light jacket and layers are useful. Some days in March still call for a winter coat.

Travel tip

Full bloom lasts about a week. If your dates are fixed, choose a destination further south for early blossoms or further north for late ones. Everywhere is crowded during the holiday period from late April to early May.
