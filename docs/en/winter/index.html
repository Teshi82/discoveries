Explore by season

Winter

Snowscapes and hot springs

Snow piled on thatched farmhouses, and open-air baths with a view of it falling. With its clear air, winter is also the best season for seeing Mt. Fuji.

Highlights

  1. Shirakawa-go (Gifu)

    A World Heritage village of steep thatched farmhouses, deep in snow. The evening illuminations on selected days in January and February require reservations.

  2. Niseko (Hokkaido)

    From December to March, skiers come from around the world for its light, dry powder snow.

  3. Ginzan Onsen (Yamagata)

    Snow piles up on a street of wooden inns, and at dusk, when the gas lamps are lit, it feels like another world.

  4. Jigokudani Monkey Park (Nagano)

    The place to see wild Japanese macaques soaking in a hot spring. Allow about 30 minutes on foot along a snowy forest trail from the entrance.

Trip notes

Best time
December – February
Where to go
Shirakawa-go (Gifu) · Niseko (Hokkaido) · Ginzan Onsen (Yamagata)
What to wear
A warm coat, gloves and shoes with good grip. Interiors are well heated, so layers you can take off easily work best.

Travel tip

Many shops and attractions close from around 29 December to 3 January. Snow can delay trains and flights, so leave some slack in your schedule.
