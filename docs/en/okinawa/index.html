Southwest Islands

Okinawa & the Kerama Islands

Southern islands of crystal-clear sea and Ryukyu culture.

Wrapped in a subtropical climate, Okinawa is a chain of islands that developed its own culture as the Ryukyu Kingdom. With Naha as your base, the trip opens out to the Kerama seas by boat, the nature of the main island's north, and the Yaeyama Islands further south.

Highlights

  1. Kerama Islands

    From Tomari Port in Naha, a high-speed boat reaches Tokashiki Island in about 35 minutes and Zamami Island in about 50. In these national park waters you may find yourself swimming with sea turtles.

  2. Shurijo Castle Park

    The site of the Ryukyu Kingdom's royal castle. The main hall was lost to fire in 2019 and has been under reconstruction; check the official site for what is currently open.

  3. Okinawa Churaumi Aquarium

    Famous for the giant Kuroshio Sea tank, where whale sharks and manta rays swim. It is in the north of the main island, about 2 hours by car from Naha.

  4. Ishigaki and Taketomi Islands

    The Yaeyama Islands, about an hour's flight from Naha. Expect the view over Kabira Bay and, on Taketomi, a water buffalo cart ride past red-tiled houses.

Trip notes

Best time
You can swim in the sea from around April to October. The rainy season runs from early May to late June, and typhoons are most common from July to October. January to March is whale-watching season.
Getting there
About 2 hours 45 minutes from Tokyo (Haneda) to Naha. From Naha Airport, the Yui Rail monorail reaches the city centre in about 15 minutes.
How long to stay
3–5 days. Add a day or two if you stay on an outer island.

Travel tip

Boats can be cancelled because of typhoons or high waves, so schedule island crossings early in your trip. The sun is strong: bring sun protection, and never touch the coral.
