6 days

Hokkaido road trip

Drive through flower fields and hills, then on to the untouched wilderness of Shiretoko, a World Natural Heritage site.

Head east from Sapporo on a drive where the scenery shifts from flower fields to lakes to primeval forest. The total distance is around 600 km. Returning your rental car at Memanbetsu Airport saves you from retracing the route.

Itinerary

  1. Sapporo (day 1)

    See the city and try miso ramen and seafood. Pick up your rental car the next morning.

  2. Furano and Biei (days 2–3)

    About 2 hours by car from Sapporo. Take your time over the lavender fields, the patchwork hills and the Blue Pond.

  3. Lake Akan (day 4)

    A long drive of about 4 hours from Biei. Stay in the lakeside hot-spring town, known for its marimo moss balls, and encounter Ainu culture.

  4. Shiretoko (days 5–6)

    About 2 hours 30 minutes from Lake Akan. Experience untouched nature on a walk around the Shiretoko Five Lakes or a sightseeing cruise. Memanbetsu Airport is about 2 hours from Utoro.

Trip notes

Best time
Flowers are at their best from late June to August (lavender in July). Autumn colours come from late September to October.
Getting around
By rental car. Japan drives on the left. To drive with a foreign licence you need an International Driving Permit; for some countries, an official Japanese translation of the licence is required instead.

Travel tip

Ezo deer and red foxes may run onto the road at dawn and dusk. Do not feed wild animals. Petrol stations are scarce on some stretches, so fill up early.
