5 days

Setouchi art and islands

A prayer for peace, a great torii standing in the sea, and bridges and art linking the islands.

A route east from Hiroshima along the Seto Inland Sea. Every day brings something different: a city walk through history, a shrine on the sea, bridges crossed by bicycle, and art on an island.

Itinerary

  1. Hiroshima (day 1)

    Visit the Peace Memorial Park and the Atomic Bomb Dome for a day of reflection on peace. In the evening, try Hiroshima-style okonomiyaki.

  2. Miyajima (day 2)

    About 1 hour from Hiroshima Station by train and ferry. See Itsukushima Shrine and its great torii, and take the ropeway up Mt. Misen for the view.

  3. Onomichi and the Shimanami Kaido (day 3)

    About 1 hour 30 minutes by train from Hiroshima to Onomichi. Walk the town of slopes and temples, then cycle across the bridges to the islands.

  4. Naoshima (days 4–5)

    Travel via Okayama to Uno Port, then about 20 minutes by ferry. Tour the museums and outdoor works and stay on the island.

Trip notes

Best time
April to May and October to November, when the weather is mild.
Getting around
A combination of trains, ferries and rental bicycles.

Travel tip

Many of Naoshima's museums close on Mondays, and some require reservations. The scenery at Miyajima changes with the tide, so check a tide table before you go.
