More than scenery

Hot springs

Hot springs well up all over the country. Bathe with a view of the snow, or meet wild monkeys soaking in the water.

Volcanic Japan has close to 3,000 hot-spring resorts. Strolling through an onsen town and spending a night enjoying the baths and dinner at an inn is a special part of any trip.

Key points

  1. Kusatsu Onsen (Gunma)

    The steaming Yubatake hot-water field sits in the centre of town, and you can watch yumomi, the tradition of cooling the water by stirring it with wooden paddles.

  2. Beppu Onsen (Oita)

    It produces more hot-spring water than anywhere else in Japan. The "Hells of Beppu" tour of vividly coloured springs is popular.

  3. Kinosaki Onsen (Hyogo)

    An onsen town where you stroll along a willow-lined river in yukata and wooden sandals, visiting seven public bathhouses.

  4. Jigokudani Monkey Park (Nagano)

    Watch wild monkeys bathing at close range. For baths meant for people, head to nearby Shibu Onsen or Yudanaka Onsen.

Trip notes

Travel tip

Wash before getting into the bath, and keep your towel out of the water. Tie up long hair. Some facilities do not admit guests with tattoos, so check in advance for private baths or places that accept them.
