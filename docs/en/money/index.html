Travel essentials

Payment

Cards and mobile payments are widely accepted, but small rural shops, temples and shrines may take cash only.

The currency is the yen. Cashless payment is spreading, but there are still situations where only cash works. It is best to carry both.

Key points

  1. Cash

    You may need cash at small restaurants, for admission fees at temples and shrines, and on rural buses.

  2. ATMs

    ATMs at convenience stores and post offices let you withdraw yen with cards issued overseas.

  3. Cards and mobile payment

    Accepted almost everywhere at hotels, department stores and chain shops. More and more shops and transport services support contactless payment.

  4. Tax-free shopping

    Visitors from overseas are eligible for a consumption tax exemption. Since November 2026, you pay the tax-inclusive price at purchase and receive a refund after checks at departure. Ask in store about the procedure.

Trip notes

Travel tip

There is no tipping custom. When paying, place your money or card on the small tray at the register.

Official information

Check the official sites for the latest opening hours, fees and transport information.
