Explore by season

Summer

Festivals, fireworks and cool highlands

Fireworks light up the night sky and festivals fill the streets. To escape the heat, head for higher ground or further north — Kamikochi or Hokkaido.

Highlights

  1. Kamikochi (Nagano)

    A mountain resort at about 1,500 m. Its heart is Kappa Bridge, with views of the clear Azusa River and the Hotaka peaks. Private cars are not allowed; go by bus or taxi.

  2. Aomori Nebuta Festival

    From 2 to 7 August every year, huge illuminated floats parade through the city at night. The dancers leaping to the chants are part of the show.

  3. Furano (Hokkaido)

    The lavender fields turn purple from mid to late July. Breezy hills in bloom are the image of a Hokkaido summer.

  4. Nagaoka Festival Fireworks (Niigata)

    One of Japan's great fireworks displays, held on 2 and 3 August on the banks of the Shinano River. Seats must be bought in advance.

Trip notes

Best time
July – August
Where to go
Kamikochi (Nagano) · Aomori Nebuta Festival · Furano (Hokkaido)
What to wear
Breathable clothing, a hat and sunscreen. A light layer is handy for air-conditioned interiors. The highlands are cool in the morning and evening.

Travel tip

The rainy season on Honshu lasts from June to around mid-July. It gets very hot afterwards, so drink water often and mix in indoor sights during the day. Watch for typhoons in August and September.
