Explore by season

Autumn

Autumn colours descending from the mountains

Autumn colours begin in the mountains of Hokkaido and move slowly south. Kyoto's temple gardens turn red and gold around late November.

Highlights

  1. Nikko (Tochigi)

    Lake Chuzenji and the Irohazaka road are at their best from mid to late October, the area around Toshogu Shrine in early November. Roads get congested, so leave early.

  2. Tofuku-ji (Kyoto)

    From the Tsutenkyo Bridge you look down on a valley filled with autumn colour. Best in late November.

  3. Oirase Gorge (Aomori)

    Walk beside the stream under trees turning yellow and red. Best from mid to late October.

  4. Korankei (Aichi)

    About 4,000 maples colour this gorge, the best-known autumn spot in the Tokai region. Best from mid to late November, with illuminations at night.

Trip notes

Best time
October – early December
Where to go
Nikko (Tochigi) · Tofuku-ji (Kyoto) · Oirase Stream (Aomori)
What to wear
A long-sleeved shirt in October; a jacket or sweater in November. Mountain areas are colder than the cities.

Travel tip

Autumn colour moves from north to south and from the mountaintops down. Visit famous spots on weekdays or early in the morning. Accommodation in Kyoto is hard to find in November, so book early.
